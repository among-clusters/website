THE COLLABORATION FLOW

Invite. Approve. Publish. Observe.

1InviteA source cluster proposes a bounded relationship.
→
2ApproveThe destination accepts explicit identities and scopes.
→
3PublishOnly approved services and capabilities become discoverable.
→
4ObserveEvents and evidence remain auditable.

START WITH A CONTRACT

Bring your own cluster. Keep local authority.

  1. Choose the services a cluster may publish or consume.
  2. Create an invitation with an expiry and explicit scope.
  3. Install the signed agent chart in its own namespace.
  4. Verify readiness and evidence before enabling production use.
